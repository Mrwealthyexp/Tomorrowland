The Blueprint

Phase by phase — how tomorrowland gets built.

Phase I · Now
Foundation
Launch the digital homeland: Pioneer Wall, Sovereign Registry, and the Educational Academy with 24 core texts and 6 study paths.
Phase II · Q1
Capital
Activate the Community Treasury on a real Polygon multi-sig address. Wire wallet SDKs (MetaMask, WalletConnect, Coinbase) and publish the transparency dashboard.
Phase III · Q2
Commerce
Open the marketplace for Black-owned businesses, with treasury-backed microgrants and community-vetted listings.
Phase IV · Q3
Governance
Launch the voting interface — one registered member, one voice. Proposals, delegation, and treasury allocation decided on-chain.
Phase V · Q4
Regional Hubs
Anchor physical chapters in cities across the diaspora — study circles, markets, and mutual aid linked back to the digital homeland.
